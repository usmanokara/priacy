Request Account Deletion

We respect your privacy and provide you with the ability to request deletion of your VION account and associated personal data.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and may not be reversible once completed.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to info@vionapp.net using the email address associated with your VION account.
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  3. Include the email address or account identifier associated with your VION account.
  4. Our team will verify the request and process the deletion.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When your account deletion request is approved and processed, we will delete or anonymize personal information associated with your account, where applicable.

  • Account information, such as your email address and name if provided.
  • Account-related profile information.
  • Personal data associated with your VION account.
  • Account-related usage information where applicable.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary for legitimate legal purposes, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or financial and accounting obligations.

Where information must be retained, it will be limited to what is legally or operationally necessary and will be deleted when the applicable retention requirement expires.

Subscriptions and Payments

VION subscriptions and purchases are processed through Apple App Store, Google Play, or other applicable payment providers.

Deleting your VION account does not automatically cancel a subscription managed through Apple or Google. Please cancel your subscription separately through the relevant App Store or Google Play account to prevent future renewal charges.

Processing Time

We will review and process account deletion requests within a reasonable period after verifying the request. Processing time may vary depending on the information involved and applicable legal requirements.
