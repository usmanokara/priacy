Account & Data Deletion – Learnaroo

This page explains how you can request deletion of your Learnaroo account and associated personal data in accordance with applicable data protection laws and Google Play requirements.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data using one of the following methods:

  • Email us at: joshnealeapps@gmail.com
  • Use the subject line: “Delete My Account”

Please include the email address or identifier used in the app so we can accurately locate your account.

If available, you may also delete your account directly from within the app settings.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • User account information (e.g., name, email, login details)
  • App usage and interaction data associated with your account
  • Any data stored on our servers related to your account

Note: Data stored locally on your device can be removed by uninstalling the app.

Data Retention Exceptions

We may retain certain data if required for legal, security, or fraud prevention purposes. Any retained data will be limited to what is strictly necessary and handled in accordance with applicable laws.

Processing Time

Once your request is received, we will process it within 7–14 business days. You will receive confirmation once your account and data have been deleted.
