Account & Data Deletion – Learnaroo

This page explains how you can request deletion of your account and associated data from the Learnaroo app.

How to Request Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data using one of the following methods:

  • Email us at: info@learnaroo.app
  • Send a request with subject: “Delete My Account”

Please include the email address or identifier used in the app so we can locate your account.

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • User account information (if applicable)
  • App preferences stored on server (if any)
  • Any associated usage history (if applicable)

Note: Some data stored locally on your device can also be removed by uninstalling the app.

Processing Time

Once your request is received, we will process it within 7–14 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once deletion is completed.

Automatic Data Removal

If your account is inactive or deleted, any related data stored on our systems will be permanently removed from our servers.

Contact
